# Local model selection and scientific-agent strategy

Advisory checkpoint, 2026-11-20 (America/New_York). This records the PI's
questions about replacing GPT-OSS and accelerating discovery. It does not
authorize downloads, dependencies, inference, new scientific jobs, changes to
running campaigns, or a new mechanism branch. The mechanism roadmap remains
the single scientific execution plan.

**Follow-on PI decision:** deploy and benchmark Apache-2.0 Mistral Small 4
on azalea, retaining GPT-OSS as the matched baseline. The private agent project
holds the approved 14-case development/held-out comparison and bounded-control
contract. This supersedes the initial no-deployment advisory scope for that
specific model/runtime comparison; it does not qualify either model or enable
unrestricted scientific execution. The recommendation analysis below is retained
as the decision rationale, including why Medium's license was avoided.

## Recommendation and its limits

The quality-oriented candidate is **Mistral Medium 3.5 128B**, using its official
FP8 checkpoint and high reasoning effort. This is a candidate for a controlled
comparison, **not an established improvement in enzymology or QM/MM reasoning**.
General agent/coding benchmarks do not establish scientific competence.

Its Modified MIT license excludes employers/companies with global consolidated
monthly revenue above USD20 million unless separately licensed. Institutional
eligibility must be resolved before deployment. Do not assume research use
exempts an employer from that condition.

If that license is unsuitable, the practical Apache-2.0 candidate is
**Mistral Small 4 119B-2603**, an MoE model with approximately 6.5B active
parameters per token, official FP8 weights, reasoning and tool calling. Its
official model card includes a two-GPU tensor-parallel example. Its chemistry
advantage over GPT-OSS is also unmeasured. "Small" is its product name, not
its total weight count. Gemma 4 31B was already tried by the PI without a
convincing result; repeating that deployment is not the priority.

The NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Super card discloses synthetic data generated by
Qwen/DeepSeek and a Qwen-derived reward model. It must not be assumed eligible
merely because NVIDIA publishes it. The main NVIDIA architecture is not
thereby a Qwen fine-tune; the PI's employer restriction may distinguish these
cases. It is excluded from the immediate recommendation pending eligibility
clarification, without downloading or running it.

## Measured local constraints

A read-only audit at 2026-11-21T03:14:53Z found two RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell
Max-Q Workstation GPUs on azalea, each 97,887 MiB (about 95.6 GiB). The topology
reports NODE, a PCIe/host-bridge route, without NVLink. The GPT-OSS service
had no resident model at that instant. This is an observation, not a promise
that the GPUs remain available.

At one byte per parameter, 128B weights require roughly 128 GB decimal, and
119B roughly 119 GB, before unquantized components, scales, KV cache, activations
and runtime workspace. Thus dual-GPU FP8 serving is plausible by memory
arithmetic; it has **not been tested** on azalea. BF16 of either full model
exceeds combined GPU capacity. Model-card multi-GPU examples do not certify
this exact hardware. PCIe communication can limit dense-model throughput;
start with one request and a modest context rather than assuming 256k is useful.
Exact weight bytes, runtime support and peak memory require a deployment test.

The current GPT-OSS baseline used official-family gpt-oss:120b, digest
`714d86958b6e870a9320e78126ac1ce3477727a1cd9bf7194b8ace0d84c62fd8`,
MXFP4, Ollama 0.30.10, 65,536 context and 32,768 output-token limits. The private
lead audit documents critical chemistry and energy-definition errors despite
successful process/schema checks. Adding specialist prompts to the same model
did not supply independent validation.

## What published successful systems actually do

1. **Co-Scientist (Nature 2026)** uses Gemini agents for evidence-grounded
   hypothesis generation, critique and ranking. Expert-guided experiments
   supply validation; its antimicrobial-resistance example recapitulated
   unpublished human findings. It does not demonstrate autonomous QM/MM
   mechanism validation. [Primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10644-y).
2. **Autonomous enzyme engineering (Nature Communications 2025)** couples
   a GPT-4-turbo interface to protein/ML models and the engineered iBioFAB
   experimental workflow. Four weeks of variant construction and screening
   yielded reported 16-fold/26-fold activity improvements for two enzymes,
   using 446/448 variants. Researchers established the assays and modified a
   modeling approach after adverse experimental correlations. This is a
   valuable direct comparator for engineering, not a demonstration of resolving
   a new atomistic enzyme mechanism. [Primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-61209-y).
3. **Coscientist (Nature 2023)** combined GPT-4, documentation, code and laboratory
   interfaces in bounded chemistry demonstrations. Instrumentation, reagents
   and parts of physical handling were supplied by researchers.
   [Primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06792-0).
4. **A-Lab (Nature 2023, corrected 2026)** used bespoke robotics, XRD, databases,
   thermodynamics and specialized ML. Corrected reporting supports 36/57 target
   materials after 353 experiments over 17 days; four previously reported
   successes became inconclusive. This illustrates both substantial parallel
   experimental investment and the value of independent reanalysis.
   [Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06734-w),
   [correction](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09992-y).
5. **AlphaEvolve** couples Gemini-generated programs to automated execution
   and scoring. Its evaluators can directly reject incorrect candidates.
   Formal proof systems offer an even sharper correctness test. Our force,
   Hessian and endpoint checks validate a stationary point on a chosen model
   surface, not its correspondence to the experimental enzyme.
   [Primary account](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaevolve-a-gemini-powered-coding-agent-for-designing-advanced-algorithms/).

The inference is that a trustworthy feedback loop, a bounded objective and
substantial repeated testing explain much of the apparent gap. These reports
do not establish that selecting the strongest chat model solves arbitrary
mechanistic chemistry, or that local hardware reproduces their performance.

## Diagnosis of this study

- **Prompting:** The PI has supplied detailed, scientifically appropriate
  direction. Longer prompts are not the main missing ingredient. Compact
  source-linked state and retrieval can improve reliability without asking the
  PI to repeatedly restate history.
- **Model capability:** Our local review made material errors about the
  nucleophile, ASP/ASH identities, literature and energy definitions. This is
  direct evidence that the current local workflow is unqualified for autonomous
  scientific planning. A replacement must be tested, not assumed superior.
- **Hardware:** The available GPUs are sufficient for useful model trials and
  substantial classical MD. More GPUs alone do not resolve protonation,
  substrate orientation, QM-region choice, CPU-bound implementations or
  uncertain experimental constraints. Scheduling and solver choices matter.
- **Execution:** Historical all-MM positional restraints, proton-switching
  reaction coordinates, restart-continuity errors, structural-preparation
  errors and excessive branching are avoidable workflow failures. They cannot
  all be attributed to the difficulty of Nyl12 or to the user's instructions.
- **Science:** Nyl12 maturation/activity is established, but elementary
  chemistry and the modeled L4-to-two-L2 reaction are not experimentally
  established. Multiple proton inventories, binding directions, water networks
  and flexible substrate/protein ensembles complicate an underconstrained
  inverse problem. Observed hydrolysis does not require a stable TI1 minimum
  on every model Hamiltonian, nor validate our four-membered route.

Determining the mechanism and engineering improved catalysis are different
deliverables. A small, well-controlled design/assay campaign can proceed without
waiting for a complete mechanistic paper. Tier A occupancy and AF3 confidence
remain filters, not validated measurements of catalytic efficiency.

## Proposed bounded next steps; no new campaign launched

1. Compare **one eligible replacement** with the retained GPT-OSS baseline on
   10–20 reviewed project cases, including held-out examples. Score critical
   chemical/source errors, appropriate next-calculation selection, valid tool
   requests, review time, wall time and peak memory. Historical failures form
   development cases, not secretly reused held-out tests. Require zero critical
   errors on this bounded gate before limited promotion; passing is not a
   guarantee of general reliability. Do not add models/personas indefinitely.
2. Close one validation loop using an appropriately small, published Ntn-
   hydrolase benchmark with accessible coordinates/characterized stationary
   points. Demonstrate reactant and product minima, a relevant first-order
   saddle and downhill connectivity with the intended toolchain. A proposed
   NylC cartoon alone is not such a numerical positive control. This is a
   narrowly scoped diagnostic, not a new full homolog-mechanism project.
3. Preserve the existing Nyl12 campaigns. Select the next mechanism calculation
   from their accepted results using an explicit hypothesis, one changed
   variable, measured cost and a discriminating endpoint. Do not equate a
   low-looking NEB maximum with verified chemistry or force TI1 to exist.
4. After model/interface qualification and PI approval, give mechanism-agent
   bounded experimental discretion: allocate US extensions by overlap/ESS/
   uncertainty; compare proton/water/pose networks; select a preapproved
   restart/repair action; and execute a small approved comparison matrix.
   Deterministic tools enforce charge, mapping, connectivity, provenance,
   restraints and resource limits. This is substantive research assistance,
   beyond extraction, while the PI retains decisions about scientific scope.

Success for the agent is an independently verified scientific milestone per
unit researcher time, not a longer report, a larger ensemble or more jobs.
No API key, new server or dependency was introduced for this advisory work.
