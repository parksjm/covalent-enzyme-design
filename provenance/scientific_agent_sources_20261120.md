# Primary-source audit — 2026-11-20

Inference: The apparent speed comes largely from bounded objectives, mature measurement/automation, many parallel trials, and reporting selected validated successes. These papers do not establish autonomous discovery of an enzyme QM/MM mechanism or validation of a new QM/MM potential.

1. Co-Scientist, Nature, 2026-05-19 (initial preprint 2025-02-26): https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10644-y . Gemini 2.0 agent coalition generates, critiques and ranks hypotheses from scientist-specified goals/literature. All experimental validation involved expert guidance and wet-lab prioritization. AML: 3/5 expert-selected drugs inhibited viability; of 3 additional novel candidates, KIRA6 active but leflunomide/nanvuranlat limited in MOLM-13. Combination effects depend on cell context. AMR example is rediscovery of an unpublished experimentally established result, not prospective autonomous experimental discovery. Self-Elo/expert preference is not chemical ground truth. Transfer: literature-grounded competing hypotheses + external evidence gates; no inference that a local small model or agent debate replaces QM/MM validation.
2. AI-Assisted Drug Re-Purposing for Human Liver Fibrosis, Advanced Science, 2025: https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/advs.202508751 . Human researchers assessed 25 drugs in established human hepatic organoid assays; two AI-recommended epigenomic modifiers and three previously characterized drugs active. Vorinostat reduced induced chromatin structural changes by 91%, an organoid endpoint, not patient cure. Closed Co-Scientist weights/code; authors expect cloud APIs. Transfer: invest in one trustworthy assay and defined efficacy/toxicity readouts before screening hypotheses.
3. Coscientist, Nature, 2023-12-20: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06792-0 . GPT-4 chat-completion planner uses web/documentation, Python, cloud lab/liquid-handling APIs for six demonstrations including known Pd cross-couplings and reaction optimization. Instrument documentation and available reagents/action space supplied; plates moved manually in local chemistry setup. Authors explicitly describe (semi-)autonomous design/execution. Not general enzyme mechanism discovery. Transfer: narrow, typed, prevalidated tool interfaces with measured analytical feedback.
4. A-Lab, Nature, 2023-11-29, corrected text: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06734-w . Human-selected air-stable targets from DFT databases, literature-trained recipe model, thermodynamic active learning, bespoke robots/furnaces/XRD; 353 experiments/17 continuous days, 36/57 targets currently accepted. Humans preload precursors/consumables. This is specialized ML and automation, not evidence that frontier-chat-model reasoning solved arbitrary science. Transfer: campaign-level denominator, immutable inputs and explicit failed targets.
5. A-Lab author correction, online 2026-01-19 (print 2026-02-05): https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09992-y . Primary author copy: https://bartel.cems.umn.edu/sites/bartel.cems.umn.edu/files/2026-02/author-correction_for_an_autonomous_laboratory_for_the_accelerated_synthesis_of_inorganic_materials.pdf . Corrected novelty means new to platform, not necessarily science. Manual peer-reviewed PXRD reanalysis supported 36/40 reported successes; four inconclusive; training-data target excluded. Transfer: independent reanalysis can reduce apparent success and is part of discovery cost.
6. Autonomous enzyme engineering, Nature Communications, 2025-07-01: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-61209-y . GPT-4-turbo API user interface, protein-language/epistasis models and low-N supervised ML coupled to engineered iBioFAB DBTL workflow. Four rounds/four weeks screened 446 AtHMT and 448 YmPhytase variants; reported 16-fold/26-fold activity, 90-fold preference improvement. Requires sequence and quantifiable fitness assay; platform/mutagenesis/assays engineered by researchers. Authors modified phytase training approach after negative experimental correlation, and no second-round new AtHMT single mutants beat WT. Not all intervention-free or all variants successful. Transfer: optimize a defined phenotype using experiments; does not resolve proton inventory, reaction path, barrier convergence, or parameter validity in Nyl12 QM/MM.
