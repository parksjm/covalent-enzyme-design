Contact rows below use only the persistent replicas from stable PA6 variants, and contacts are measured in Tier B frames with a 4.0 Å heavy-atom cutoff.
Interaction type is inferred from residue chemistry and ligand group, so these are putative interaction classes for visualization and presentation, not quantum-mechanical assignments.

## Interpretation guidance for partial PA6 variants

It is not wrong to perform the same contact analysis for partial PA6 variants, but it is risky to present those contacts as robust favorable interactions. With only one persistent replica, contacts may be replica-specific and can easily become a cherrypicked explanation. The safer use is diagnostic: compare the passing replica against the failed replicas to ask what interactions are missing, unstable, or only transient.

For presentation, stable-variant contacts are suitable as candidate stabilizing interactions; partial-variant contacts should be labeled as hypotheses or failure-mode diagnostics.
